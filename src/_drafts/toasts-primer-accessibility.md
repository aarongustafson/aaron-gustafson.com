I’m glad to see a design system ask whether we need a toast before explaining how to build one. Primer’s guidance walks through alternatives for everything from a completed action to a failed form submission, rather than treating a little disappearing rectangle as the answer to all of them.

The distinction between simple and complex actions is especially useful. Seeing the issue you just created may be confirmation enough; a bulk operation might need a persistent summary. And if something failed, people need time to find the message, understand it, and work out what to do next. We shouldn’t make that a race.
