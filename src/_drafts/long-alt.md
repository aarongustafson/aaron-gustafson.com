If your image’s alternative text is turning into an article, Adrian Roselli has some useful demonstrations of what that experience can be like. His example piles a cat’s description, history, citations, and multiple languages into one `alt` attribute, then tests what browsers and screen readers do with it.

The issue isn’t finding a magic character limit. It’s that we’ve put content somewhere people can’t navigate it the way they could a document with paragraphs, headings, and links. I’d much rather put that detail in the page and let the image’s alternative do its more focused job. The information can still be available without making someone listen through all of it to get past a cat.
