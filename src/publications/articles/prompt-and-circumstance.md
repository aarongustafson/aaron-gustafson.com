Co-authored with Yumeng Ma, Jacob O. Wobbrock, Joe Devon, Michael Fairchild, Jennifer Mankoff, and Venkatesh Potluri. We introduce PACE (Prompt Accessibility Controlled Evaluation), a testbed for evaluating accessibility in HTML form components generated by large language models. Across 26,100 outputs, the study examines how accessibility varies with the model, component, prompt construction, and evaluation measure.
