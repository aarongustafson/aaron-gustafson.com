If your image’s alternative text is turning into a novella, Adrian Roselli wants you to know what that experience can be like. In this post he stuffs the `alt` attribute, then tests what browsers and screen readers do with it.

Spoiler: the experience is awful on all counts, but you should really read it though to fully grasp the horror.
