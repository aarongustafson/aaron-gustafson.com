I find a lot of cool links throughout the week and I usually bookmark them on some service, like [Pinboard](http://pinboard.in/u:aarongustafson), but for some reason I never considered posting them to the blog. I’m recitfying that as of today.

Here are last week’s finds:

- [HTML5 Pattern](http://html5pattern.com/)\
  A user-generated collection of helpful regular expressions for using HTML5’s form validation `pattern` attribute.
- [Mobile Web Design 2.0](http://dl.dropbox.com/u/4322454/MWD-2nd-Edition.pdf) (PDF, 61K)\
  Cameron Moll’s tongue-in-cheek second edition of his popular mobile guidebook.
- [Design and UX Books I Have Loved (Articles, Too!)](http://rosenfeldmedia.com/uxzeitgeist/lists/zeldman/design-and-ux-books-i-have-loved-articles-too)\
   Zeldman recounts his favorites for Rosenfeld Media’s blog. A few of mine are in there.
- [CSS Summit](http://environmentsforhumans.com/2011/css-summit/)\
  3rd Annual CSS conference from Environments for Humans.
- [Designing for Content: Creating a Message Hierarchy](http://webstandardssherpa.com/reviews/designing-for-content-creating-a-message-hierarchy/)\
  Steph Hay’s inaugural piece for [Web Standards Sherpa](http://webstandardssherpa.com). Hint: she started out with a bang.
- [Monkey steals camera to snap himself](http://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/newstopics/howaboutthat/8615859/Monkey-steals-camera-to-snap-himself.html)\
  Pure craziness.
