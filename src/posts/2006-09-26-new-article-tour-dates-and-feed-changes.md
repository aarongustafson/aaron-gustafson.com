Hello, my name is Aaron Gustafson and I’m a delinquent blogger. It’s been over a month since my last <del>confession</del><ins>post</ins>.

<!-- more -->

If it makes any difference, I’ll say that I am sorry, I’ve just been a little busy of late. Those of you keeping up with me via [my Flickr stream](http://www.flickr.com/photos/aarongustafson/) or [Plazes](http://beta.plazes.com/user/aarongustafson/) will see why: lots of travelling. It doesn’t seem to be ending anytime soon, but I have a bit of downtime today so I thought I’d post some updates.

First off, I have [a new article up on Digital Web Magazine that is all about the `<button>`](http://www.digital-web.com/articles/push_my_button/). If you spend as much time with web forms as I do, I highly recommend checking it out. For some of you, the techniques may seem like old hat, but there are a lot of people out there who still haven’t realized the real power of the `<button>` element. On another publishing note, I’ve gotten a promotion to Technical Editor at [<cite>A List Apart</cite>](http://alistapart.com), which is sweet. Many thanks to Erin, Jeffrey and the rest of the team.

Also, if you haven’t been to this site lately or don’t follow [my events feed on Upcoming.org](http://upcoming.org/syndicate/v2/my_events/12412){type="application/rss+xml"}, I will be speaking at [The <abbr title="Asynchronus JavaScript and XML">AJAX</abbr> Experience](http://www.theajaxexperience.com/) in Boston late next month and I have also been booked to speak as part of [Web Directions North](http://north.webdirections.org/) in Vancouver early next year. I will also be co-leading a one-day workshop with [Malarkey](http://stuffandnonsense.co.uk/) while I’m there, if you’re up for some serious CSS-meets-DOM scripting magic.

Finally, I’m starting to do some tidying up with regard to my feeds. I’ve had a [FeedBurner](http://feedburner.com) account for ages, but hadn’t ever really used it until today. I’ve created [a new feed for this site](http://feeds.feedburner.com/EasyReader){rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml"} which also incorporates [my ma.gnolia bookmarks](http://web.archive.org/web/20080109075207/ma.gnolia.com/people/aarongustafson/bookmarks) and Flickr photos (which, of late, have seen a lot more attention than this site). For the three of you using the old feed, I’d appreciate it if you could move over to this new one as I may disable the old feed at some point.

Anyway, back into the fray… I’m sorry for not writing more often.
